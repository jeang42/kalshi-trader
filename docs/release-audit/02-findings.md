Status values: **Verified** (reproduced as reported), **Partially verified**
(real, but narrower or different than reported), **Not reproduced**.
Every remediation below has a regression test; see `05-test-results.md`.

| P0-D | Medium-High | **Verified** (bounded) | `pm/venue/kalshi_orders.py` | `_today_submit_count()` (audit-log scan) → check → submit was a non-atomic read-check-act across processes; two daemons (e.g. reactive-scalper + reactive-hold) could both observe `cap-1` and both submit. | Concurrent processes near the daily cap overshoot it by up to the number of concurrent submitters.
