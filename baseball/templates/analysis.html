🛠️ Programmatic access

The join queries are exposed for ad-hoc analysis: 

from baseball import analysis

# What Kalshi tickers correspond to one game?
analysis.tickers_for_game(game_pk=823463)

# Book trajectory around a specific play
analysis.book_around_play(game_pk=823463, play_id=42, window_minutes=5.0)

# Aggregate reaction stats across all HRs
analysis.reaction_summary("Home Run", window_minutes=10.0, min_pct_change=0.02)
